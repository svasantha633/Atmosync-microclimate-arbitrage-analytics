# AtmoSync Data Analysis: Insights Report

## Executive Summary

SQL data analysis on 8,127 daily micro-zone records across 5 NCR cities (Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad) from January 1 to August 31, 2026.

## Major Business Insights

1. **May Peak Season**: May 2026 was the top-grossing month (₹6.68 Crore, +17.97% MoM growth) driven by heatwave temperatures averaging 35.86°C.
2. **Stockout Loss Concentration**: Ice Cream (25.6%) and Cold Drinks (24.6%) account for **50.2% of all unfulfilled lost revenue** (₹16.67 Lakhs lost combined).
3. **Micro-Zone Risk Clusters**: Sector 150 (Noida), South Delhi (Delhi), and Cyber City (Gurugram) experience the highest stockout rates and revenue leakage.
4. **Rainfall Volatility**: Rainy days cause a **50.2% drop in daily revenue per zone** compared to Hot & Sunny days.
5. **Weekend Surge**: Sunday and Saturday sales average 734 and 724 units/day (12.6% higher than weekday averages).

---

## Key Recommendations

- Reallocate safety stock to top stockout-vulnerable micro-zones (Sector 150, South Delhi, Cyber City).
- Execute Friday evening inventory replenishment pushes for weekend surges.
- Calibrate purchasing triggers with 3-day weather forecast APIs for heatwaves and rain events.
